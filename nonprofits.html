Comprehensive Coverage

Nonprofit Funding Capabilities

Every facet of the grant development pipeline managed with professional rigor.

Funding Types We Pursue

  • Private & Family Foundations: Identifying family trusts with aligned mission statements.
  • Community Foundations: Regional discretionary and donor-advised funds (DAFs).
  • Corporate Philanthropy & CSR: Aligning local branch grants and national corporate grant initiatives.
  • Government Grants (Local, State, Federal): Navigating complex NOFO/RFP requirements and federal portals.
  • General Operating Support (GOS): Securing unrestricted institutional capital to strengthen operational runway.
  • Capacity-Building Grants: Technology upgrades, strategic planning, staff development, and board governance.

How We Accelerate Your Mission

  • Eliminate Speculation: Stop wasting hundreds of staff hours on low-fit applications.
  • Professional Funder Positioning: Frame your programs with clear logic models and SMART goals.
  • Budget Narrative Alignment: Ensure every requested dollar directly maps to narrative activities.
  • Sustainable Pipeline: Maintain a 12-month calendar of rolling submissions to diversify revenue.
  • Post-Award Compliance: On-time, compliant progress reports that encourage multi-year renewals.
