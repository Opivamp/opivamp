Eligible Business Grant Areas

  • Equipment & Infrastructure Grants: Funding to purchase machinery, software, or specialized facilities.
  • Workforce Development Subsidies: State programs that subsidize new employee onboarding and skill certification.
  • Innovation & Commercial R&D: Public funds supporting technological advancement and domestic manufacturing.
  • Community Economic Development: Grants for enterprises bringing employment to distressed or revitalizing corridors.
  • Corporate Innovation Contests: Private sector corporate prizes awarding non-dilutive capital and pilot contracts.
  • Export & Trade Promotion: Federal subsidies assisting businesses entering international export markets.

The OPIVAMP Advisory Advantage

  • Eligibility Filtering: We rigorously screen out predatory contests and focus only on legitimate institutional grants.
  • Commercial Case Framing: We present your revenue model, market demand, and ROI with executive clarity.
  • Public-Private Alignment: We show how your business growth satisfies the awarding agency's public economic goals.
  • Streamlined Effort: You focus on running your business while we handle narrative development and compliance.
  • Zero Equity Dilution: Retain 100% ownership and control over your company's future.
