Strategic Architecture

Our Two-Stage Core Process & Beyond

How OPIVAMP transforms organizational goals into prioritized opportunities, high-scoring proposals, and sustainable funding momentum.

1. RESEARCH → 2. APPLICATION → 3. AWARD → 4. REPORTING → 5. NEXT OPPORTUNITY
Phase 01: Identification & Screening

Find the Right Opportunities

"We begin by understanding your organization, funding needs, programs, goals, eligibility, geography, and priorities. We then research and evaluate funding opportunities that genuinely align with those factors."

Every engagement begins with thorough discovery. Rather than jumping into application writing blindly, we conduct an analytical assessment of your legal status, geographic reach, operating budget, and target population.

Why We Ask Thorough Discovery Questions: The questions we ask during the discovery process — including funding needs, programs, eligibility, organizational priorities, and goals — are part of this first phase. They allow us to determine which opportunities are genuinely worth pursuing.

Phase 1 Deliverables

  • ✓ Organization & program assessment
  • ✓ Funding goals & budget need analysis
  • ✓ Comprehensive eligibility screening
  • ✓ Funder background & 990-PF giving analysis
  • ✓ Verified opportunity identification
  • ✓ Submission deadlines & cycle windows
  • ✓ Award range & average grant sizes
  • ✓ Explicit application requirements checklist
  • ✓ Strategic fit & probability assessment
  • ✓ Prioritized funding pipeline recommendation

Phase 2 Deliverables

  • ✓ Application strategy & narrative roadmap
  • ✓ Full grant narrative development
  • ✓ Clear program description & activity schedule
  • ✓ Data-backed community needs statement
  • ✓ SMART goals & measurable objectives
  • ✓ Impact measurement & evaluation plan
  • ✓ Long-term sustainability section
  • ✓ Organizational capacity & team profiles
  • ✓ Budget narrative & line-item justification
  • ✓ Supporting documents & attachment prep
  • ✓ Final technical compliance review
  • ✓ Submission verification support
Phase 02: Proposal Development

Turn Opportunities Into Strong Applications

"Once suitable opportunities are identified and prioritized, we move into application development. Each proposal is developed around the specific funder's priorities rather than using generic, recycled narratives."

Grant evaluation panels grade submissions against detailed rubrics. Our writing process focuses on aligning every section with what reviewers specifically reward: measurable outcomes, sound financial justifications, and demonstrable community need.

We collaborate with your leadership to extract authentic organizational stories while structuring the proposal within rigorous technical guidelines.

Phase 03: Post-Award Stewardship

Beyond the Application

"Successful funding relationships require more than winning an award. We continue supporting organizations with reporting, impact documentation, funder communications, and future funding strategy."

The most cost-effective grant to secure is the grant you already have. Funders who receive thorough, transparent milestone reports and clear impact data frequently renew awards or invite subsequent larger grant requests.

OPIVAMP helps maintain active dialogue with funders between grant cycles, documenting outcomes so that multi-year partnerships flourish.

Phase 3 Deliverables

  • ✓ Milestone tracking & expenditure compliance
  • ✓ Interim progress reports
  • ✓ Annual impact & final grant reports
  • ✓ Quantitative outcome documentation
  • ✓ Funder relationship updates
  • ✓ Grant renewal & extension strategy
  • ✓ Next-tier funding opportunity pipeline
Diversified Capital Architecture

Don't Build Your Funding Strategy Around One Grant.

"One application can create one possibility. A strategic funding pipeline creates multiple possibilities."

Where appropriate, we recommend pursuing 5 or more aligned opportunities. The goal is not to submit applications for the sake of volume — it is to create a diversified pipeline of genuinely relevant opportunities.
