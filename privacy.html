Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At OPIVAMP, we recognize that funding strategy, organizational budgets, donor lists, intellectual property, and proprietary program metrics represent highly sensitive institutional assets. We adhere to the highest standards of professional discretion. All data submitted via our assessment tools, discovery forms, or advisory sessions is treated as strictly confidential.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information when you interact with our website or submit consultation requests, including:

  • Contact details (name, email address, telephone number)
  • Organizational attributes (entity name, tax-exempt status, website URL, annual operating budget)
  • Programmatic narratives and funding goals shared for advisory evaluation
  • Self-assessment responses generated via our Funding Readiness Tool

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is utilized solely to:

  • Evaluate organizational eligibility and alignment with prospective grant opportunities
  • Prepare customized advisory proposals and engagement schedules
  • Coordinate consultation appointments and provide strategic feedback
  • Deliver agreed-upon research deliverables, proposal drafts, and reporting documentation

4. Zero Data Monetization

OPIVAMP does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade client information, contact databases, or organizational metrics to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of client records and proposals.

6. Inquiries & Data Rights

If you have questions regarding our data confidentiality protocols, or wish to review, update, or delete information submitted through our site, please contact Peter Oyedemi directly at opivamp@opivamp.com.
