Frequently Asked Questions

Honest, straightforward answers about how we evaluate opportunities, structure proposals, scope pricing, and partner with organizations.

We work with two core client categories: Nonprofit organizations (including 501(c)(3) public charities, community foundations, youth development programs, faith-based initiatives, educational institutions, health organizations, and human services providers) and For-Profit organizations (including small businesses, early-stage startups, social enterprises, women-owned and minority-owned businesses seeking non-dilutive grants, workforce subsidies, and innovation funding).

Yes. While their organizational models differ, both nonprofits and businesses can benefit immensely from institutional funding. We tailor our advisory specifically: for nonprofits, we focus on philanthropic foundations, corporate CSR, and public service grants; for businesses, we focus on non-dilutive economic development, equipment, workforce, and commercial R&D grants.

No. OPIVAMP does not guarantee grant funding, and organizations should be cautious of any agency or freelancer that does. Funding decisions are made completely independently by external grant review panels, trustees, and government awarding officers based on changing budgets, competing applicant pools, and internal priorities. What OPIVAMP guarantees is rigorous eligibility screening, tailored funder alignment, evidence-backed narrative architecture, and adherence to professional standards that give your application its strongest competitive positioning.

We utilize comprehensive institutional databases, IRS Form 990-PF philanthropic disclosures, state economic agency portals, federal clearinghouses (such as Grants.gov), and proprietary sector research. We don't just rely on keyword searches; we cross-reference a funder's historical grant sizes, geographic giving boundaries, and past recipient profiles.

We evaluate opportunities against our 5-point alignment filter: legal eligibility, geographic service area, alignment with the funder's explicit strategic priorities, feasibility of matching fund requirements, and award size relative to your operational capacity.

No. We are a full-cycle funding consultancy. Our work includes Phase 1 opportunity research, strategic funder alignment, readiness assessments, annual pipeline calendar planning, and post-award grant reporting, impact measurement, and renewal support.

Phase 1 includes an organizational discovery assessment, funding goals analysis, multi-tiered eligibility screening, funder historical giving analysis, verified opportunity profiles, submission deadlines, average award ranges, application requirements matrices, and a recommended priority pipeline.

Phase 2 includes narrative architecture, problem/needs statements backed by demographic data, SMART goals and measurable objectives, program design descriptions, evaluation methodologies, sustainability plans, organizational capacity narratives, budget narrative line-item justifications, and pre-submission compliance reviews.

Our Research Package starts at $50 per aligned funding opportunity, delivering verified eligibility, funding history, and strategic fit analysis. Multi-opportunity portfolios can be bundled for comprehensive annual pipelines.

Our Application Package starts at $150 per grant application for standard foundation and corporate proposals. More complex federal solicitations or specialized NOFOs are scoped individually based on narrative depth and documentation requirements.

Yes. In Phase 3 (Post-Award Support), we assist clients with milestone tracking, interim progress reports, annual impact documentation, and funder communications that set the stage for multi-year grant renewals.

Phase 1 (Opportunity Research & Alignment) generally takes 1 to 2 weeks. Proposal development in Phase 2 typically takes 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the complexity of the funder's application portal and the turnaround time for your organizational attachments.
