Category 01

Grant & Funding Research

"We identify funding opportunities based on your organization, eligibility, objectives, programs, geography, funding needs, and strategic priorities."

Randomly applying for every grant found on search engines leads to severe resource waste. OPIVAMP conducts exhaustive multi-tier research into private foundations, corporate philanthropic arms, and governmental awarding bodies to isolate opportunities where you are genuinely competitive.

What We Investigate:

  • ✓ Geographic boundaries
  • ✓ Giving history patterns
  • ✓ Typical award amounts
  • ✓ Application window cycles
  • ✓ Board affiliations
  • ✓ Prior recipient profiles

Service Deliverables

  • Grant Opportunity Research: Scans across thousands of public and private funding databases.
  • Foundation Research: Analysis of family, regional, and national philanthropic entities.
  • Government Funding Research: Identification of local, state, and federal grant notices.
  • Corporate Funding Research: Corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs and giving funds.
  • Eligibility Analysis: Rigorous pre-screening against organizational legal status and programmatic focus.
  • Funder Alignment Scoring: Matching your objectives with published funder strategic priorities.
  • Deadline Tracking: Comprehensive submission calendars with rolling and fixed deadlines.
  • Funding Pipeline Development: Structured portfolio of vetted opportunities prioritized for execution.

Application Deliverables

  • Grant Application Preparation: Complete end-to-end proposal documentation.
  • Letters of Inquiry (LOI): Crisp, high-impact introductory concept letters.
  • Concept Papers & Executive Summaries: Briefs for foundation program officers.
  • Grant Narratives: Tailored, persuasive storytelling articulating organizational competence.
  • Needs Statements: Rigorous community data and demographic evidence justifying the request.
  • Goals & Objectives: SMART milestones that facilitate clear evaluation criteria.
  • Evaluation Plans: Quantitative metrics, qualitative indicators, and logic models.
  • Sustainability Sections: Post-grant continuation models funders expect to see.
  • Budget Narrative Support: Clear line-item financial justifications matching narrative activities.
  • Application Review & Submission: Final technical quality check and portal submission support.

Category 02

Grant Application & Proposal Development

"Turning vetted opportunities into rigorous, funder-centric proposals that stand out in competitive review panels."

Grant reviewers read dozens of applications with limited time. Generic descriptions and recycled boilerplate are immediately apparent and often disqualified. We develop custom narratives around each funder's explicit scoring criteria, presenting your work with intellectual clarity and emotional resonance.

We ensure every component — from the needs assessment and methodology to the budget narrative and sustainability plan — operates as a cohesive, compelling business case for investment.

Category 03

Funding Strategy & Pipeline Planning

"Building a strategic funding infrastructure designed for long-term organizational stability."

A one-time grant win can solve an immediate operational deficit, but without a proactive strategy, the organization will face the same cliff the following year. OPIVAMP structures annual funding roadmaps that schedule multiple submissions across diverse funding sources.

We evaluate your organizational capacity, match upcoming initiatives with the correct funding types (operating vs. program vs. capacity), and position your leadership for productive funder dialogue.

Strategic Deliverables

  • Funding Strategy Development: Comprehensive multi-year funding roadmap.
  • Grant Pipeline Planning: Prioritized sequence of upcoming foundation and public deadlines.
  • Funder Prioritization Matrix: Ranking opportunities by probability, effort, and award size.
  • Annual Funding Calendar: 12-month calendar preventing deadline congestion.
  • Grant Readiness Assessment: Benchmarking organizational foundation before application expense.
  • Organizational Positioning: Framing unique differentiators within your sector.
  • Program-to-Funder Alignment: Matching specific program budgets with funder giving buckets.

Post-Award Deliverables

  • Interim & Annual Progress Reports: Formal compliance documentation for award managers.
  • Impact Reporting: Translating program outputs into compelling outcomes and stories.
  • Funder Updates: Strategic communications maintaining funder engagement between awards.
  • Outcome Documentation: Structuring data collection tools to satisfy grant requirements.
  • Sustainability Planning: Demonstrating how funded initiatives transition into ongoing operations.
  • Post-Award Compliance Support: Adherence to expenditure timelines and reporting deadlines.

Category 04

Grant Reporting & Post-Award Support

"Grant writing does not end when an application is submitted — stewardship is what creates multi-year renewals."

Winning a grant creates an obligation to deliver on proposed promises. High-performing organizations treat grant reporting as an opportunity to build trust, demonstrate accountability, and set the stage for subsequent grant renewals or increased funding tiers.

OPIVAMP ensures that your reporting is submitted on time, complies with all funder guidelines, and documents programmatic outcomes with analytical precision.
